# Quest Family Decision Log

One row per **behavioral** change to the family (criterion C6): a change to what a skill
does — flow, gates, options, boundaries, schemas. Mechanical and structural edits are not
logged here; git history covers them. Maintainers and the audit only.

Ids are `qdNNN`, unique and never reused. They are deliberately not `dcNNN`: `dc` numbers
are campaign-scoped decision constraints and restart in every campaign.
